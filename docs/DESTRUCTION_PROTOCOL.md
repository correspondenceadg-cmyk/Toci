# Destruction Protocol

NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1 "Destroy" checklist for the D4 path.

## Tools

3–4 mm drill bit, drill, safety glasses, non-flammable surface, fire-safe container.

## Steps

1. Power off. Wait 60 seconds.
2. Locate the storage IC. Model-specific ASCII diagram from the app.
3. Drill through the die. Two holes, offset, through the storage IC only.
4. Place in the fire-safe container for 30 minutes.
5. Log completion in the app.

## Confirmation

Each step is checked off. The app logs completion locally. The log is destroyed with the device.
